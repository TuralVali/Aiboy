Hardening recommendations

  • Guard every denominator or define zero-denominator behavior.
  • Validate numeric types before arithmetic.
  • Test empty DataFrames with and without expected columns.
  • Test pandas alignment with mismatched indexes and columns.
  • Check dtype and shape after pandas/NumPy transformations.
  • Reject or explicitly convert numeric-looking strings.
  • Enforce domain invariants such as probabilities remaining in [0,1].
